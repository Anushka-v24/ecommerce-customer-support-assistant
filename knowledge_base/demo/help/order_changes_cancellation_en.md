# DEMO ONLY — fictional guide: changing or cancelling an order

This invented test content describes a pretend order-change process at the fictional Example Shop. It is not a real retailer's cancellation or refund policy, and asking the assistant about it does not cancel an order.

## Check your order status first

What you can change depends on whether your order has been dispatched. Ask the assistant for the status of your order using your order ID (format ORD-1234). An order marked Processing has not been dispatched yet. An order marked Shipped is on its way, and Delivered means it has arrived.

## Before dispatch (Processing)

While an order is still Processing, you can ask to:

- Cancel the whole order or a single item.
- Change the delivery address, as long as the new address is in our delivery area.
- Change the delivery method, where available.

Item, size or colour changes are not possible on an existing order; cancel the item and place a new order instead.

## After dispatch (Shipped)

Once an order has been shipped it cannot be cancelled or changed. You can:

- Refuse the delivery when the courier arrives; the parcel is returned to us and treated like a cancellation.
- Accept it and request a return after delivery. In this fictional example, returns can be requested within 30 days of delivery for unopened items. Some products have their own return window or conditions.

## Refunds after a cancellation

For prepaid orders (UPI, card, net banking or PayPal), the refund goes back to the original payment method. In this fictional example, refund processing usually takes 5-10 business days; the exact time can vary by product. Your bank or payment provider may take a little longer to show it on your statement. Cash on delivery orders that are cancelled before delivery have nothing to refund. You can ask the assistant for your refund status at any time using your order ID.

## How to request a change or cancellation

Tell the assistant your order ID and what you want to change or cancel. If the change needs a member of the support team, the assistant will confirm with you and raise a ticket, and you can follow it in the My Tickets tab.
