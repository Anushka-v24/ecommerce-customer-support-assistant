# DEMO ONLY — fictional guide: account help

This invented test content describes pretend account features at the fictional Example Shop. It is not a real retailer's account or security policy. Never share a password or OTP with anyone, including the assistant.

## Creating an account

Select "Sign up" and enter your name, mobile number and email address. We send a one-time password (OTP) to your mobile number to verify it. Enter the OTP, then set a password. You can also create an account during checkout.

## Signing in

You can sign in with your mobile number or email address and your password, or with your mobile number and an OTP. If you have several accounts, make sure you use the one your orders were placed from.

## OTP problems

If you do not receive an OTP:

- Check that the mobile number is entered correctly, without extra digits.
- Wait a short while; messages can be delayed by your network.
- Check that your phone has signal and is not blocking messages from unknown senders.
- Use "Resend OTP". In this fictional example an OTP is valid for 10 minutes, and only the latest OTP works.

If you have tried several times and still cannot sign in, ask the assistant to connect you with the support team. Never share your OTP with anyone who contacts you.

## Forgotten password

Select "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your registered mobile number or email address. Follow the OTP or reset link to set a new password. Choose a password you have not used before. If you no longer have access to your registered mobile number or email, contact the support team.

## Updating your phone number or email

Go to your account profile and choose "Edit" next to the phone number or email. We send an OTP to the new number or a verification link to the new email to confirm the change. Your existing orders stay linked to your account.

## Managing addresses

You can add, edit or delete delivery addresses in the Addresses section of your account and choose a default address. Changing an address in your account does not change the address on orders already placed. To change the address on an order that has not been dispatched yet, see the order changes guide or ask the assistant.

## Deleting your account

You can ask for your account to be deleted from your account settings or by asking the support team. Before deletion, please make sure you have no orders in progress, open returns or pending refunds. Deleting your account removes access to your order history, saved addresses and reviews and cannot be undone. Some records may be kept where the law requires, for example invoices.
