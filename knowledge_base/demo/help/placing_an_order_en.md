# DEMO ONLY — fictional guide: how to place an order

This invented test content describes a pretend shopping flow at the fictional Example Shop. It is not a real retailer's checkout process, price, or delivery promise.

## Step 1: Find a product

Use the search bar at the top of any page and type a product name, such as "Laptop Pro" or "headphones". You can also browse by category. Open a product page to see its price, stock status, warranty and delivery options. If a product shows as out of stock, the page may show an expected restock date; you cannot order it until it is back in stock.

## Step 2: Add it to your cart

On the product page, choose a quantity and select "Add to cart". You can keep shopping and add more items. Open the cart icon at any time to review what you have added, change quantities or remove items. Adding an item to your cart does not reserve it; stock is only confirmed when you place the order.

## Step 3: Check out

From the cart, select "Proceed to checkout". You will be asked to sign in, or to create an account if you do not have one. Then:

1. Choose or add a delivery address. We currently deliver only within India.
2. Choose a delivery method. In this fictional example: Standard delivery (3-5 business days, ₹49, free on orders above ₹499), Express delivery (1-2 business days, ₹149), or Same-day delivery (same day for orders placed before 12 noon, ₹199, only in Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru).
3. Apply a discount code if you have one. Demo codes are not redeemable.
4. Review the order summary, including item prices and delivery fee.

## Step 4: Pay

In this fictional example the store accepts UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, PayPal, and cash on delivery. Select a method and follow the on-screen steps. For cards, UPI and net banking you may be asked to approve the payment in your bank or UPI app. If a payment fails, no order is placed; you can try again or choose another method.

## Cash on delivery (COD)

Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹10,000. Choose "Cash on delivery" at the payment step and pay the courier when your parcel arrives. Please keep the exact amount ready if possible. Orders above ₹10,000 must be paid online.

## Order confirmation

After a successful order you will see a confirmation page with your order ID, in the format ORD-1234. Keep this order ID: you need it to track the order, ask about a refund, or raise an issue. You can see all your orders and their status (for example Processing, Shipped or Delivered) in your account. An invoice is emailed after dispatch and can be downloaded from the order details page in your account.

## Need help while ordering?

If something goes wrong at checkout, tell the assistant what you were trying to do and any error message you saw. If you were charged but did not get an order ID, share the payment date and method so the support team can check it.
