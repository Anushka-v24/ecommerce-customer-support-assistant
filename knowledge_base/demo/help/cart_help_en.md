# DEMO ONLY — fictional guide: cart help

This invented test content describes a pretend shopping cart at the fictional Example Shop. It is not a real retailer's cart rules.

## Adding items

On any product page, choose a quantity and select "Add to cart". A small number on the cart icon shows how many items are in your cart. Out-of-stock products cannot be added; the product page may show an expected restock date instead.

## Changing quantity or removing items

Open your cart and use the plus and minus buttons to change the quantity of an item. To remove an item, select "Remove". The order total and delivery fee update automatically. Standard delivery is free on orders above ₹499 in this fictional example, so removing items can bring a ₹49 delivery fee back.

## Saved for later

If you are not ready to buy an item, select "Save for later" in your cart. It moves out of your cart into a Saved for later list below it, and is not included in your order total. Select "Move to cart" when you want to buy it. Saved items are not reserved, and their price or stock may change.

## Cart limits

In this fictional example:

- You can add up to 10 units of the same product to one order.
- Cash on delivery is available only when the order total is ₹10,000 or less. Larger orders must be paid online.
- Items in your cart are not reserved. Stock and prices are confirmed only when you place the order.

## Cart is empty or items disappeared

Your cart is linked to your account. If items are missing, check that you are signed in to the same account you used before. An item may also be removed automatically if it goes out of stock. If items keep disappearing, tell the assistant what happened.

## Price changed in my cart

Prices and offers can change between adding an item and checking out. The price shown at the final review step before payment is the price you pay.
