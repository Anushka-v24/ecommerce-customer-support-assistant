# DEMO ONLY — fictional payments FAQ

This invented test content describes pretend payment terms at the fictional Example Shop. It is not a real retailer's payment policy. For refund timelines, see the separate demo refund policy.

## Which payment methods are accepted?

In this fictional example the store accepts UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, PayPal, and cash on delivery. You choose the method at the payment step of checkout.

## Is cash on delivery available?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹10,000. For larger orders, please pay online with UPI, card, net banking or PayPal.

## My payment failed. What should I do?

If a payment fails, no order is placed. Check your card details, UPI app or bank balance and try again, or choose a different payment method. If money was deducted but you did not receive an order ID, it is usually reversed automatically by your bank. If it does not appear back in your account, tell the assistant the payment date, amount and method so the support team can check it.

## I was charged twice

Tell the assistant your order ID and the date and amount of each charge. The support team will check whether two orders were placed or whether one charge is a pending authorisation that your bank will release.

## Where is my invoice?

An invoice is emailed after dispatch and can be downloaded from the order details page in your account.

## How do refunds reach me?

Refunds for prepaid orders go back to the original payment method. Ask the assistant for your refund status at any time using your order ID (format ORD-1234).

## Is it safe to share my card details?

Only enter payment details on the checkout page. Never share your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or OTP in chat, by phone or by email, including with the assistant or anyone claiming to be from support.
