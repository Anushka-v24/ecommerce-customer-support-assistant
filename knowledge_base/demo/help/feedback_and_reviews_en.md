# DEMO ONLY — fictional guide: feedback, reviews and suggestions

This invented test content describes a pretend feedback process at the fictional Example Shop. It is not a real retailer's review policy.

## Writing a product review

You can review a product after your order has been delivered. Open the order in your account, select the item, and choose "Write a review". Give a star rating from 1 to 5 and, if you like, a short written review and photos. Reviews help other shoppers, so focus on the product itself: quality, fit, performance and whether it matched the description.

## Review guidelines

To keep reviews useful, please:

- Review only products you have bought and used.
- Avoid personal details such as phone numbers, addresses or order IDs.
- Avoid offensive language.
- Keep delivery or refund complaints out of product reviews; raise those as an issue instead so the support team can act on them.

Reviews that break these guidelines may not be published. You can edit or delete your own review from your account.

## Feedback about your experience

If you want to share feedback about delivery, packaging, the website or the support you received, just tell the assistant. For example: "I want to give feedback about my delivery." Positive feedback is welcome too and is shared with the team.

## Feedback versus complaints

Feedback is for telling us how we did. If something went wrong with an order and you need it fixed, such as a damaged, wrong or missing item, please raise an issue instead. That creates a support ticket with a ticket number you can follow in the My Tickets tab.

## Suggestions

Have an idea for a product we should stock, a feature for the website, or a way to improve our service? Tell the assistant and describe your suggestion. We read every suggestion, although we cannot reply individually or promise that a suggestion will be adopted.
